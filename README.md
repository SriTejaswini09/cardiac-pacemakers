## 📝 Overview

This portfolio explores:

- **Evolution of Pacemakers:** From bulky 1950s tabletop units to modern MRI-conditional, leadless devices.  
- **Types of Pacemakers:** Single-chamber, Dual-chamber, Biventricular (CRT), Rate-responsive, Leadless, CRT-D.  
- **In-Depth Sections:**  
  - Single-chamber & Dual-chamber device design  
  - Biventricular pacing in heart failure  
  - General block diagram & subsystem functions  
  - Electrical safety, EMI resistance, and MRI-compatibility  
  - Regulatory standards (FDA, ISO, IEC) & safety testing protocols  
- **Design Aesthetic:** Warm earth-tone palette, Helvetica typography, transparent heart background.
