About

Cardiac pacemakers are implantable devices that help regulate abnormal heart rhythms by delivering electrical impulses to the myocardium. Since their invention in the 1950s, pacemakers have undergone a major evolution—from bulky external devices to compact, programmable, biocompatible implants...

Modern pacemakers have dramatically improved life expectancy and quality of life for patients with arrhythmias...

Introduction to Pacemaker Types

Pacemakers can be classified based on the number of chambers they stimulate and how they synchronize heartbeats...

  • Single-Chamber Pacemaker: Stimulates only one chamber of the heart...
  • Dual-Chamber Pacemaker: Coordinates pacing between the atrium and ventricle...
  • Biventricular Pacemaker: Also known as CRT...
